# How to Write a Cover Letter That Gets Read

---

A cover letter explains why you're a good fit for a specific role — something your resume can't do alone. For entry-level candidates, it addresses the experience gap and shows genuine interest.

## The Structure (4 Paragraphs)

### Paragraph 1: Why This Role
State which position you're applying for and one specific reason you're interested.

### Paragraph 2: What You Bring
Connect 2–3 experiences or skills to the job requirements with specific examples.

### Paragraph 3: Why This Company
Show you understand their work and explain why it matters to you.

### Paragraph 4: Closing
Thank them, express enthusiasm, provide contact details.

## Formatting Guidelines

| Element | Guideline |
|---------|-----------|
| Length | One page max, 250–400 words |
| Font | Match your resume (Inter, Calibri, Arial, 11–12pt) |
| File name | FirstName_LastName_CoverLetter.pdf |
| Format | PDF |
| Greeting | "Dear Ms. Patel" or "Dear Hiring Team" |
| Tone | Professional but human |

## What to Draw On Without Experience

- **Academic projects** — describe like work projects
- **Internships** — even short ones count
- **Extracurriculars** — leadership, event planning, volunteering
- **Relevant coursework** — mention briefly
- **Self-directed learning** — online courses, personal projects, hackathons

## The 5 Biggest Mistakes

1. **Making it about you, not them** — Focus on what you bring to their team
2. **Being too generic** — Use specific examples with numbers
3. **Repeating your resume** — Add context, don't summarize
4. **Writing too much** — One page maximum, edit ruthlessly
5. **Not customizing** — Change company name, role, and at least one connecting paragraph

## Checklist Before Sending

- [ ] Opening mentions the specific role and company
- [ ] 2–3 concrete examples connecting your background to the job
- [ ] Something specific about the company researched
- [ ] Under one page
- [ ] Professional but not robotic tone
- [ ] Proofread for spelling and grammar
- [ ] File named properly
- [ ] Same font/header as your resume
