## Section 2: Our Mission

> **Gutenberg blocks:** Kadence Row Layout (narrow width, centered) → Heading + Paragraph

### Content:

**Heading (H2):**  
Our Mission

**Paragraph:**  
Starting a career is one of the most important transitions in life — and one of the least supported. University prepares you academically, but rarely teaches you how to write a resume that gets read, how to perform in an interview, or how to make decisions about your professional path.

ClearPath exists to fill that gap. We offer straightforward career resources and one-on-one guidance that help early-career professionals take confident first steps.
